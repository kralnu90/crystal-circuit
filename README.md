# Crystal Circuit

*Roll the board. Fight the fates. Out-play your rivals.*

A board game and an action RPG in one, from Kralnu Games. Race three AI rivals round a board for 30 rounds, fight in real time whenever the Fate Die calls for it, and play cards to get ahead.

## [▶ Play the work in progress](https://kralnugames.com/crystal-circuit/)

- **Story mode.** The Prologue, *Felix's Journal*: eight short chapters set in the world of Aldmere that teach the game one system at a time. You enter Varek Voss's Emberglass Circuit in place of your sick older brother, guided by his journal.
- **Two fronts.** Roll 2d6 to move round the board (doubles roll again). Land on a tile and a 12-sided Fate Die decides what happens: a 1, 4, 8 or 12 is a fight, from easy to deadly.
- **Real-time fights.** Move, attack, guard, dodge, use your class skill and cast spells on the move. Tap Guard just before a hit for a perfect guard. Hit weaknesses to stagger enemies.
- **Six classes.** Warrior, Monk, Mage, Ranger, Lancer and Guardian, each with its own attack and skill. Make as many heroes as you like.
- **Three rivals with their own decks.** Everyone holds a hand of five cards and can play two a turn: hostile cards, hidden traps, Ward and Mirror to defend, and Pass the Buck to hand someone else your fight. Rivals hold grudges against whoever hurts them.
- **Something to aim for.** Bosses on rounds 10, 20 and 30, and prizes for the top three in distance travelled and in damage dealt.
- **The Colosseum.** Practice any fight with any class, level and gear, take your hero into cup tournaments between circuits, or run the Gauntlet for essences to craft your class's signature item.
- **Gear that matters.** Weapons and armour with traits and linked gem sockets, merchants whose stock changes every visit, and a 15-card deck you build from cards you win.

Works offline once installed. Saves stay on your device.

This is an early build. Things will change, and saves may reset between versions.

## Changelog

### 1.7.0 · 18 November 2026
- **Story mode: the Prologue, Felix's Journal.** A new Story button on the main menu. Eight short chapters, each opening with a page from Felix Galloway's journal and teaching one part of the game: moving and doubles, the Fate Die, fighting, guarding and dodging, magic, gem links, cards and rivals, and the Colosseum.
- New story foes: the **Lock Warden** (slow, honest wind-ups, and it counters button-mashers) and **Mire Shells** (shrug off steel, weak to your spells).
- **Sable Rook**, Felix's old training partner, is your first rival in the Emberford Qualifier.
- Progress and rewards are per hero. Rewards are modest and paid once per chapter. You can replay any chapter, reread the journal, or skip the prologue and still collect the starter rewards.
- A circuit you already have in progress is kept safe while you play a chapter.
- New heroes start on the Story screen.

### 1.6.0 · 17 November 2026
- **Pixel-art heroes.** All six classes now have proper sprites in battle, with idle, walking (front, back and side), attacks, hurt and skill animations. Gauntlet opponents use them too, so you can tell a Lancer from a Monk at a glance.

### 1.5.1 · 17 November 2026
- **Ranger rebalance.** Arrows deal about 20% less damage, and 30% less again at point-blank range (the bow can't be drawn fully, shown by greyer arrows), so staying at range is how Ranger is meant to play.
- **Volley** arrows deal about 40% less each: still great against a group, no longer a single-target nuke up close.

### 1.5.0 · 17 November 2026
- **Rival aggression setting.** Choose Calm, Normal or Ruthless rivals when you start a circuit. Calm rivals play hostile cards and traps far less and pick on each other more than on you; Ruthless rivals gang up on the leader.
- **Gear to aim for.** Merchants only sell weapons and armour up to your level. Higher-level gear still shows in stock, marked with the level you need, so you can see what's coming.
- **Steeper prices at the top.** Early gear costs about the same, but top-tier kit now costs far more (Crystal around 2,000 gil, Dragonbone and Dragon Scale over 4,000), so it's a goal across several circuits.
- Fixed the update notice repeating after updating.

### 1.4.0 · 17 November 2026
- **Gauntlet mode.** Ten duels in the Colosseum against heroes of the other classes, who guard, dodge, close the gap and use their class skills. The last two fights are two against one. Costs 1 ticket; unlocks after a Silver Cup win.
- **Objectives.** Every Gauntlet fight has a random objective (first strike, no damage, stagger, Limit finish, no items, under 45 seconds, skill x3, weakness x3, guard x3). Complete it and win to bank that class's essence.
- **Cash out or fall.** Cash out after any win to keep your essences. Fall once and the run is wiped. Clear all ten for a Gauntlet Seal.
- **Crafting.** A new Crafting menu on the main menu, in the in-game hub and in the Colosseum. Craft your class's signature item from essences, a Boss Core (boss rounds), Bronze and Silver Laurels (cup wins) and a Gauntlet Seal.
- **Signature items** sit in their own slot and change your class skill: Whirlwind pulls enemies in, Chi Dash chains, Meteor splits, Volley pierces, Jump staggers, Shield Bash reflects projectiles.

### 1.3.0 · 17 November 2026
- **Colosseum Cups.** Tournaments for your real hero, fought between circuits. Spend a ticket and pay the entry fee, then win round after round. After each win, take the purse and leave or push on. Lose and you keep half; win the final for the cup prize.
- **Three cups:** Bronze (5 rounds, Warden final), Silver (6 rounds, Gatekeeper final, Lv 5 or a Bronze win) and Crystal (7 rounds, Crystal Tyrant final, Lv 10 or a Silver win; costs two tickets).
- **Tickets:** earn one for every circuit you finish and another for topping a podium. Hold up to three.
- **Balanced against the board:** HP carries between rounds, used potions are gone, purses pay at most normal fight gil, EXP is capped, and cups are locked while a circuit is in progress.
- Prizes go straight to your hero: gil, EXP, new cards for your deck, gems and rare finds.

### 1.2.0 · 17 November 2026
- **The Colosseum.** Practice fights from the main menu. Pick any class, your level (1 to 40), any weapon and armour, the gems in every socket, a forge level and how many potions and ethers to bring.
- **Choose your foe:** Easy to Deadly groups, the four bosses, or a duel with a single enemy, at any enemy level you like, in any region and on any difficulty.
- **No rewards, no risk.** Nothing you do in the Colosseum touches your heroes. It just remembers your best clear time against each foe.
- A Leave button to quit a practice fight early, and a results card with your time, damage dealt and damage taken.

### 1.1.0 · 17 November 2026
- **A proper main menu.** The title screen is now a set of gem-icon buttons: Grand Circuit, Custom board, Heroes, Deck, Settings, What's new, How to play, Review and Get the latest, each opening its own page.
- **Board picker with mini maps.** Every board shows a little map of its shape, its length and its region before you start.
- **Heroes page.** Switch between your heroes or make a new one from one place.
- **In-game hub.** The MENU button opens the same gem pads: Status, Cards, Rivals, Equip, Gems, Settings, How to play and Save & quit, each with a back arrow, in place of the old tabs.
- **Merchant's Rest hub.** Shop and Forge sit at the top of the hub when you stop at a merchant, so you can change gear and gems there too.
- **Settings pads.** Sound, Screen shake and Board speed are the same toggle pads on the title screen and in-game.

### 1.0.0 · 17 November 2026
- **The Fantasy Crystals overhaul.** A new look from top to bottom:
  - **The board** floats in a cosmic void: a nebula tinted for the region you're in, stars, a faint crystal grid with a light sweep, and a turning sigil in the middle showing the round (it pulses purple on boss rounds).
  - **Gem tiles:** Ruby fire, Sapphire frost, Citrine storm and Emerald wind, with a Diamond Sanctuary and Amethyst merchants. Each has its rune etched in, glints run across them, and a glowing circuit trace with beads of light links them up.
  - **Crystal pieces:** glass miniatures on rune discs, with a little figure of their class inside. They bob, turn in the light, squash on landing and leave light trails (red when reversed). Warp turns your piece into a beam of light.
  - **Status at a glance:** Ward and Mirror shields, frosted Skipped pieces, Slowed drips, Reversed arrows, a Curse eye, buffs orbiting overhead, and each rival's deck symbol on their disc.
  - **Glass top bar:** class portrait with a level badge, HP and MP bars that show what you just lost, a Limit ring that turns into a spinning prism, and gil that rolls up with a floating "+120".
  - **Tarot cards:** your hand fans out as full-art cards with type-coloured borders and rarity stars. Tap one to lift it and read it, then Play or Discard. Hostile cards now light up the rivals you can target: tap one on the board. Played cards fly to their target and burst into gem shards.
- New fonts (Cinzel and Inter), saved for offline play too.
- The title screen now always shows the right version number.
### 0.9.0 · 17 November 2026
- **The graphical overhaul.** Everything is redrawn, still all in code:
  - **Board:** raised stone tiles with icons for each region, a glowing circuit path, hills on the horizon, drifting embers, snow, rain or leaves, and proper game pieces for you and your rivals.
