# Crystal Circuit

*Roll the board. Fight the fates. Out-play your rivals.*

A board game and an action RPG in one, from Kralnu Games. Race three AI rivals round a board for 30 rounds, fight in real time whenever the Fate Die calls for it, and play cards to get ahead.

## [▶ Play the work in progress](https://kralnugames.com/crystal-circuit/)

- **Two fronts.** Roll 2d6 to move round the board (doubles roll again). Land on a tile and a 12-sided Fate Die decides what happens: a 1, 4, 8 or 12 is a fight, from easy to deadly.
- **Real-time fights.** Move, attack, guard, dodge, use your class skill and cast spells on the move. Tap Guard just before a hit for a perfect guard. Hit weaknesses to stagger enemies.
- **Six classes.** Warrior, Monk, Mage, Ranger, Lancer and Guardian, each with its own attack and skill. Make as many heroes as you like.
- **Three rivals with their own decks.** Everyone holds a hand of five cards and can play two a turn: hostile cards, hidden traps, Ward and Mirror to defend, and Pass the Buck to hand someone else your fight. Rivals hold grudges against whoever hurts them.
- **Something to aim for.** Bosses on rounds 10, 20 and 30, and prizes for the top three in distance travelled and in damage dealt.
- **Gear that matters.** Weapons and armour with traits and linked gem sockets, merchants whose stock changes every visit, and a 15-card deck you build from cards you win.

Works offline once installed. Saves stay on your device.

This is an early build. Things will change, and saves may reset between versions.

## Changelog

### 0.8.0 · 17 November 2026
- **A roster of heroes.** Make a new hero of any class without losing the others. Each keeps their own level, gear, gems, deck and any circuit in progress. Switch between them from the title screen.
- Main menu: how to play, settings (sound, screen shake, board speed), leave a review, support, install the app, get the latest version, and credits.
- Sound effects.
- Installable, and playable offline. You get a notice when a new version is ready.

### 0.7 · 17 November 2026
- **Merchants change their stock every visit:** 4 cards, 4 weapons, 4 armours, gems, accessories and items, with better gear turning up as you level.
- 15 weapons and 14 armours, most with a trait: built-in elements, lifesteal, crits, extra MP, faster stagger, elemental resistance, thorns, regeneration and more.
- New items: Hi-Potion and Elixir.

### 0.6 · 17 November 2026
- **Card decks.** You and every rival hold a hand of five from your own deck, and can play two cards a turn plus one discard. 27 cards: movement, Fate Die tricks, fight buffs, defence, hostile cards, hidden traps and board bonuses.
- Rivals have themed decks (Trapper, Speedster, Saboteur) and hold grudges. Anyone hit by a hostile card is Wary for the rest of the round.
- Deck editor. Win permanent cards from fights, bosses and podium finishes, and claim a rival's card when you top a podium.

### 0.5 · 17 November 2026
- Reverse now hits a rival, not you.
- Losing a fight now also costs 20% of your distance travelled.

### 0.4 · 16 November 2026
- Linked gem sockets on weapons and armour. Elemental + a spell adds its element to your strikes, or resists it on armour. New Spread support gem.
- Enemies attack with elements.

### 0.3 · 16 November 2026
- Tougher fights: difficulty levels, enemies that resist weapons, a perfect guard, multi-hit combos and enraged bosses.

### 0.2 · 16 November 2026
